Brand and marketing

Consumers prefer to do business with companies that are doing the right thing for our communities and our environment. Purchasing net-zero emission energy through GreenPower shows your business cares about sustainability.

Brand Guidelines

GreenPower is a trusted renewable energy accreditation. Using the GreenPower logo correctly helps show your customers, staff and stakeholders that your environmental credentials are credible.

These guidelines explain how to use the logo and brand consistently.

Brand and marketing guidelines

GreenPower has released updated brand guidelines and a new logo as of December 2025. Retailers and partners are required to update websites, marketing materials, and other resources referencing GreenPower to the new logo by 1 July 2026.

Download Brand and marketing guidelines 1.42 MB · PDF

Apply to use logo

As of 1 July 2026, if your business purchases 80% or more of its annual electricity usage as GreenPower, you are eligible to use the GreenPower logo on your marketing and business materials.
