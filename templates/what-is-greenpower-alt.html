What is GreenPower?

GreenPower helps you support Australia’s renewable energy transition with confidence. It’s a simple and credible way to purchase renewable energy for your home or business.

White Rock, Glen Innes, NSW. Image courtesy and © NSW Government DCCEEW.

Why your energy choices matter

The energy you use every day to power your home or run your business has an environmental impact. GreenPower matches your energy use with renewable electricity or renewable gas. Together we are supporting Australia’s renewable future.

  • Choose GreenPower renewable electricity
  • Choose GreenPower renewable gas

Learn more about GreenPower’s impact

GreenPower makes it easy to support renewable energy

When you choose GreenPower, your energy use is matched with renewable energy on your behalf from GreenPower-accredited projects.

Step 1: Choose a GreenPower-accredited product

Select a GreenPower-accredited renewable electricity or renewable gas product that meets your needs.

Step 2: Your energy purchase is matched

Your purchase is matched with GreenPower-accredited renewable electricity generation or certified renewable gas from GreenPower-accredited projects.

Step 3: Your purchase is independently audited

GreenPower independently audits and verifies sales to ensure claims are accurate and supported by accredited renewable energy or renewable gas.

Who is GreenPower for?

GreenPower is available for households and businesses of all sizes.

Households

For households, choosing GreenPower offers a simple way to support Australia’s renewable energy transition, with no solar panels or new equipment needed.

  • GreenPower for households

Small to medium-sized businesses

For small to medium-sized businesses, choosing GreenPower demonstrates your commitment to accredited renewable energy, helping build trust with your employees and customers.

  • GreenPower for small to medium-sized businesses

Large organisations

For organisations with sustainability targets and reporting requirements, GreenPower provides a credible, independently audited option to reduce reported electricity-related Scope 2 emissions and gas-related Scope 1 emissions.

  • GreenPower for large organisations

How GreenPower tracks renewable energy

GreenPower uses certificates to track renewable energy from generation, matching your GreenPower purchase. This creates a clear record that is independently checked. Certificates don’t change the energy delivered to your home or business. Instead, they are a record of the renewable energy matched with your GreenPower purchase. There are two types of certificates:

Renewable electricity certificates: Large-scale Generation Certificates (LGC’s)

LGC’s represent renewable electricity generated by GreenPower-accredited renewable electricity Generators. One LGC represents one megawatt hour (MWh) of renewable electricity.

Learn more

Renewable gas certificates: Renewable Gas Guarantee of Origin certificates (RGGOs)

RGGOs represent renewable gas produced by GreenPower-accredited Producers. One RGGO represents one gigajoule (GJ) of eligible renewable gas.

Learn more

Why trust GreenPower?

GreenPower is backed by government accreditation, strict standards and independent auditing. This gives you confidence that GreenPower-accredited products meet program requirements and that purchases are accurately tracked and verified.

What GreenPower is not

GreenPower is a government accreditation program that supports renewable energy.

GreenPower does not:

  • change the electricity or gas delivered to your home or business
  • build or operate renewable energy projects
  • operate as a carbon offset scheme.
