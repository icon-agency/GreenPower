    GreenPower products

    GreenPower is government-accredited renewable energy. When you buy a GreenPower product, your provider buys the same amount of electricity from accredited renewable generators on your behalf and adds it to the grid.

    Choosing a percentage

    The percentage is the share of your electricity use that is matched with GreenPower. You pay a small premium on top of your usual rate for that share.

    • GreenPower 25% — a quarter of the electricity you use is matched.
    • GreenPower 50% — half of the electricity you use is matched.
    • GreenPower 100% — all of the electricity you use is matched.

    Not every provider offers every percentage, and some offer their own steps in between. The products listed on each provider's card are the ones they currently sell.

    Read how GreenPower accreditation works

    GreenPower RGGO certificates

    A Renewable Gas Guarantee of Origin (RGGO) certificate represents a set quantity of renewable gas produced by an accredited facility and delivered into the gas network. Certificates are how renewable gas is tracked and claimed, because the gas itself cannot be separated from the network once it is in there.

    Coupled certificates

    Sold together with your gas supply. Your provider retires the certificates against the gas you use, and the renewable claim appears on your account.

    Decoupled certificates

    Bought separately from your gas supply, so you can make a renewable claim without changing gas retailer. Businesses commonly use these to cover a site or a reporting period.

    What to ask your provider

    GreenPower is sold as an add-on to an ordinary energy plan, so the details differ from provider to provider. These are worth confirming before you sign up.

    • Which GreenPower percentages can I buy on the plan I am on?
    • What does GreenPower add to my bill, per unit and per quarter?
    • Is GreenPower shown as its own line item on the bill?
    • Does adding GreenPower change my contract term, my rates or my exit fees?
    • Can I change or cancel GreenPower later, and how much notice do you need?
    • How and when will you confirm the purchase has been made?

    Providers on this page are accredited, which means their GreenPower purchases are independently audited every year.
